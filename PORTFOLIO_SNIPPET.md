## UK Road Accidents Analysis — MySQL & Power BI

Analyzed 307,973 Great Britain road accident records from 2021–2022. Cleaned and transformed date fields in MySQL, developed KPIs and investigated accident severity, time, vehicle, road, junction, weather and geographic patterns. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard and translated findings into road-safety recommendations.

**Key result:** accidents declined 11.7% and casualties declined 11.9% from 2021 to 2022.
